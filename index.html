About

I am a Senior Customer Quality Engineer at Infineon Technologies in Batam, supporting customers across Europe, America, Japan, and Korea. What I love most is solving problems end to end: using 8D to contain issues, uncover root causes, and make sure every fix truly holds.

Before Infineon, I spent about 10 years at Transcal, growing from QA Executive to QA/QC Manager. That journey gave me a strong foundation in IATF 16949 certification, process capability studies, and supplier quality for automotive and aviation seating. I later spent a year as a Senior Supplier Quality Engineer at PCI, a Celestica company, where I handled NCMR and MRB decisions. Every step has taught me something valuable.

Outside work, I enjoy building small web apps that make everyday life a little easier: Family Menu Planner for weekly family meals, Family Bills & Documents for household bills and important papers, Double Rally for organizing tennis and padel doubles, IELTS Generator for IELTS practice, and BG Remover for quick background removal from photos. I believe the best tools are simple to use and genuinely helpful.

Looking ahead, I am excited about the next chapter: a master's degree in engineering, quality, or data science, and a career in Europe where I can keep growing.
