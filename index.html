About

I am a Senior Customer Quality Engineer at Infineon Technologies in Batam, handling complaints for customers in Europe, America, Japan, and Korea. My work covers the full loop: containment, 8D, root-cause analysis, and verifying that fixes hold.

Before Infineon, I spent about 10 years at Transcal, where I moved from QA Executive to QA/QC Manager. That role covered IATF 16949 certification, process capability studies, and supplier quality for automotive and aviation seating. I also spent a year as a Senior Supplier Quality Engineer at PCI, a Celestica company, managing NCMR and MRB decisions.

Outside work, I build small, practical web apps: Belajar Paritta, a learning app for Buddhist chants, and Perencana Menu Keluarga, a family meal planner. I like tools that are simple to use and solve a real problem.
